## You may not use the Service to:

- Engage in or promote illegal activity

### Harm others

- Threaten, harass, stalk, defame, or impersonate any person
- Publish content that incites violence, depicts the sexual exploitation of minors, or promotes hate against a protected class
- Collect or process personal information of others without their lawful consent
- Send unsolicited commercial messages ("spam") in violation of CAN-SPAM, TCPA, or analogous laws
- Use the Service to direct abuse, harassment, or unfair competition at a business you do not own or have authority over

### Compromise security

- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service except as part of a coordinated disclosure with us
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to any account, system, or network
- Interfere with the Service's normal operation (DoS attacks, flooding, etc.)
- Bypass rate limits, quotas, or access controls
- Forge or manipulate identifiers, headers, or other signals to disguise origin

### Misuse the Service

- Resell the Service, sublicense it, or use it to build a competing product
- Use the Service to train or improve a competing AI or machine-learning model
- Use automated means (scrapers, bots) to extract data except via documented APIs and within published rate limits
- Use the Service to host or distribute content that violates law or this AUP
- Misrepresent the Service's outputs as something other than what they are (e.g. claiming AI-generated content is human-authored when that distinction is material)
